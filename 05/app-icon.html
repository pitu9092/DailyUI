Design Rationale

Symbolism

Sun + cloud = weather clarity. Triangular composition creates stability. Sparkles suggest live data updates.

Color Psychology

Deep blue (trust), golden sun (optimism), cyan accent (modern tech). All work on light & dark backgrounds.

Glassmorphism

Soft gradients, ambient glows, and micro-highlights create premium 3D depth without being gaudy.

Recognition

Distinctive silhouette at 16×16px. Stronger than typical weather icons. Uniquely WeatherWise.
