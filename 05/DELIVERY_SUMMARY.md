## 🎯 Unique Design Choices

1. **Unified Glassmorphism** — Not just sun + cloud, but treated consistently with gradients, glows, and highlights
2. **Balanced Asymmetry** — Sun upper-left, cloud lower-center creates dynamic yet stable composition
3. **Ambient Lighting** — Cyan outer glow ties everything together
4. **Animated with Purpose** — Sparkles suggest "live data" but don't distract
5. **12-Layer Depth System** — Multiple subtle effects create premium 3D feel
6. **Triangular Composition** — Invisible apex (sun) → base (cloud) = visual stability
7. **Breathing Space** — Left-side emptiness counterbalances right-side cluster
